# WATCH proposal template

> WAIT setups only. Approving arms a monitor; it does **not** fill a trade.

```
👀 WATCH proposal: <title>   (slug: <slug>)

Setup forming
- Symbol: <symbol>   Direction: <long|short>
- Gates already passed: <list>
- Missing event (the one thing): <e.g. 5m CISD through 1h swing low at PRICE>
- Invalidation (idea is dead): <price or condition>
- If it prints → promote to: <options | swing>

Why watch rather than take or skip
- Score: <total>/100
- Why not TAKE yet: <named event not printed>
- Why not SKIP: <structure still intact>

Reply:  approve <slug>   |   shelve <slug>: <reason>   |   modify <slug>: <change>
```
